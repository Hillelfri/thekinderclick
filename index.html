Program Rules & Requirements

Ages

We accept children ages 20 months to 3 years.

Health

Children must be fever-free for 24 hours before returning. Vaccinations must be up to date; religious exemptions are not accepted.

Meals

We serve nutritious breakfast (9:45 AM), snacks (11:45 AM), and lunch (1:45 PM). No outside food is permitted.

Naps

Daily nap time is from 11:45 AM to 1:30 PM. We currently only accommodate children who require a mid-day nap.
